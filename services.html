What we do

From the first sketch to the final finish

We don't just supply materials — we partner with builders from design through completion, bringing expertise and commitment to every phase of the project.

01
Phase one

Select the right materials

Great builds start with great decisions. In the design and planning stage, our consultants sit down with you to work through material choices, finish options, and product specifications — so nothing is left to chance when the first nail goes in.

We carry an extensive range of products and know them inside and out. Whether you're choosing between wood species for a staircase, comparing window lines for energy efficiency, or sourcing a statement front door, we bring clarity to decisions that can otherwise feel overwhelming.

One-on-one consultation with an experienced product specialist
Full product samples and specification sheets available in-store
Guidance on cost, lead time, and availability before you commit
Coordination with architects and designers when needed
Schedule a consultation
02
Phase two

Build with confidence

When it's time to break ground, Main Street is your supply partner from start to finish. We stock the lumber, structural products, and building materials serious builders depend on — and we work hard to make sure the right material is on your job site when you need it.

Our team understands job site realities. We're responsive, accurate with orders, and proactive about flagging issues before they become problems. Because a delayed delivery or wrong product can cost far more than the material itself.

Dimensional, engineered, and specialty lumber in stock
Accurate order fulfillment with attention to grade and specification
Responsive team that understands builder timelines
Builder accounts available for ongoing project relationships
Request a lumber quote
03
Phase three

The finish that makes it home

The finishing details are what separate a house from a home. Our millwork service brings precision, craftsmanship, and an eye for detail to the final phase of every project — from the front door to the crown moulding to the stair railings.

We use the latest technology and carry a comprehensive range of finish products — mouldings, stair parts, doors, and custom millwork — so your build arrives at a level of quality that builders and homeowners both take pride in.

Full range of stock and custom moulding profiles
Stair parts, newels, balusters, and treads in multiple species
Interior and exterior door packages fully configured
Custom millwork sourcing and coordination available
Talk to our millwork team
