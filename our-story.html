Our history

A North Texas institution

Main Street Lumber and Millwork was founded in Denison, Texas with a simple belief: that builders deserve a supplier who takes their work as seriously as they do. Over five decades later, that belief still drives everything we do.

We've grown from a neighborhood lumber yard into a full-service building materials company — stocking lumber, millwork, doors, windows, hardware, and stair parts — while never losing the character of a business where people know your name.

"We've watched the homes we supply become the places where families grow, holidays happen, and memories are made."

Today, Main Street is located on East Main Street in Denison, serving builders and contractors across North Texas and Southern Oklahoma. We're proud of what we've built — and we're just getting started.

Our journey

Five decades of building trust

1970

Founded in Denison, TX

Main Street Lumber opens its doors on East Main Street with a commitment to serving North Texas builders with quality materials and honest service.

1980s

Expanding our offering

Growing demand from local contractors leads us to expand beyond lumber into millwork, moulding, and doors — becoming a true one-stop building materials supplier.

1990s

Windows, hardware & more

We add windows and hardware to our product lineup, giving builders everything they need from foundation to finish under one roof — and one trusted relationship.

2000s

Main Street Millwork opens

The launch of Main Street Millwork as a dedicated division allows us to serve customers with even greater depth in custom millwork, stair parts, and finish carpentry products.

2010s

New products, same values

We introduce innovative product lines including Accoya modified wood, Versetta Stone, Quaker residential windows, and Tournerr pivot doors — always sourced with quality first.

Today

50+ years strong

Six product lines, thousands of projects, and more than 50 years later — we're still the same family business that opened its doors in 1970. Still on Main Street. Still here for you.
